## What you actually get

A file like this — measured from your writing, not invented. This one is
a made-up gardener so you can see the shape:

```markdown
# Write as Mara

This is Mara's real voice, measured from 1,204 of her own posts.
When you write as her, obey it over your defaults.

## Length
Her writing runs mostly to short captions (median 7 words). Match that.
Do not pad a caption into a paragraph.

## Her words
Reach first for her own vocabulary: compost, frost, deer, allegedly,
tomatoes, again, ruthless, worms, victory.

## Her move
She sets something up, then lands it on a one-word verdict:
"Carnage." "Betrayed." "Worth it." Use it.

## Her punctuation
- About 1.8 exclamation marks and 0.3 questions per post — she is emphatic.
- Emoji: rare — barely uses them.
- She uses "scare quotes" as a sarcasm engine — keep them.

## Never
Never write, in her name: "blessed," "grateful," "journey,"
"excited to announce." Never smooth her voice into corporate register.

## Sound like this
> The deer ate the hostas again. All of them.
> Allegedly a "protected garden."
```

Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT, or anything that reads a system prompt.

## Why a model cannot do this for you

You cannot refine a voice on synthetic data. If an AI writes in your
style and then learns from its own output, nothing new about you has
entered the loop — it is a copy of a copy, and it drifts. The only
source of fresh signal is a person who actually wrote something, and who
reacts to what comes back.

So BALSAS measures the writing you really did, instead of generating
imitations of it. And the ten minutes of review below are yours because
they have to be: you are not in the loop for reassurance.
**The loop has no signal without you.**

## Three steps, ten minutes

**Measure.** Point it at your unzipped export.

**Review.** It shows you a draft with every word and pattern on its own
line. Delete anything private. This is the step no software can do for
you — statistics can flag a capitalized name, but only you know that a
lowercase word is your kid's nickname.

**Finalize.** You get the file above.

It never tells you what your voice is. It shows you the evidence, and
you approve the portrait.

## Privacy, stated exactly

- **Local only.** No network calls, no telemetry, no account, no
  dependencies beyond Python 3.8+. Your archive never leaves your disk.
- **No passages are copied out of your posts.** The shareable file holds
  measured patterns and whatever example lines you personally approved.
- **Four independent layers of name protection**, and no single layer is
  trusted alone: automatic detection, your own redaction list, a gate
  that refuses to write a draft containing an excluded term, and your
  review.
- **Finalize is strict.** Hand it the wrong file — your private profile,
  a stray Markdown file — and it refuses by name. A filename mix-up
  cannot ship your archive.

## Worth knowing before you buy

- **Writing from before 2022 matters most.** Anything newer may already
  have been shaped by AI. Your older writing cannot have been.
- **It needs a real archive.** A few dozen posts will not produce a
  useful portrait. Hundreds will.
- **English and Lithuanian are tested.** Other languages work but are
  measured more coarsely.
- **You do the review.** Ten minutes of your attention is required, by
  design. If you want a one-click answer, this is not it.
